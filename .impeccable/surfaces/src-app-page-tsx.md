## Direction contract

THESIS: The site proves the client is understood in one screen, not sold to — it opens on the client's situation and the visible path forward, refusing the "somos especialistas em..." engineering-firm default the client explicitly banned.

OWN-WORLD: Espresso `#2B1C14` as dominant institutional ground, Pérgamo `#F5F2EC` as light respiro, Rosa Queimado `#D4748C` reserved for CTA/accent only, Bege Latão `#D2B48C` for hairlines/architectural rules, Carvão `#2D2D2D` for body text on light. Montserrat for display/headings (confident, geometric, architectural), Poppins for body/UI. Architectural grid: strong horizontal rules, numbered steps (01–05 journey), restrained cards, generous whitespace, no gradients/glass/generic icon tiles. Symbol mark used small and sparingly, never as decorative filler.

STORY: Visitor arrives with a specific situation (opening, regularizing, expanding, or reacting to an exigência). They see themselves named in "Qual é a sua situação?", understand the 5-step path (Entendemos → Identificamos → Desenvolvemos → Acompanhamos → Buscamos a conformidade), trust the tone (no alarmism, no absolute promises), and submit the short diagnostic form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky header (logo left, nav center, CTA right) over a hero with: kicker "Engenharia de Conformidade Predial", headline "Seu imóvel precisa estar apto. O caminho até lá não precisa ser complicado.", one supporting line, primary CTA "Analisar meu imóvel" (Rosa Queimado) + secondary "Falar no WhatsApp" (outline), and a right-side architectural graphic built from the journey steps (numbered marks + connecting line), not a stock photo.

FORM: Pinned by brief (client's own brand manual + site direction doc), not rolled — concept-seed tournament skipped per new-work.md §1 "Incomplete brand" (confirmed assets/positioning/structure preserved, system expanded for this surface). No seed key.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
